This is not real betting odds and this app does not accept wagers. "Win %" columns are the model's honest true estimate (from each team's own Pythagorean win% run through the Log5 formula, with a home-field adjustment derived from this league's own actual home/away split, and a modest head-to-head adjustment based on this season's series between the two specific teams -- weighted down significantly since a season series is a much smaller, noisier sample than either team's full record). The "Odds" columns apply a representative sportsbook-style vig on top of that estimate so the numbers look like an actual posted line rather than a raw probability -- that vig figure is illustrative, not scraped from any real book, and won't track FanDuel's actual current pricing. Still no real market data, no injury reports, no weather, no line movement — a statistical estimate only, and a noisy one at that given how short this league's season is.
